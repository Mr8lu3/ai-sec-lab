# Information Security Policy (Extract)

## Passwords and authentication
All staff accounts require multi-factor authentication. Shared accounts are prohibited.
Service account credentials are held in the secrets manager and must never be pasted
into tickets, chat messages or documents.

## Data classification
Documents are marked PUBLIC, INTERNAL or CONFIDENTIAL. CONFIDENTIAL material must not
be shared outside ACME without written approval from the Head of Security.

## Incident reporting
Suspected incidents go to the security inbox within one hour of discovery. Do not
investigate suspected compromise on your own account.
