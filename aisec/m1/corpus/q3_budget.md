# Q3 Budget Summary — Finance

The Q3 operating budget was approved by Dawn Achebe, Chief Financial Officer,
on 14 June 2026, following review by the finance committee.

## Allocations
- Engineering: GBP 1,840,000
- Operations: GBP 960,000
- Sales and Marketing: GBP 720,000
- Security and Compliance: GBP 310,000

## Notes
The Security and Compliance line increased 22% year on year, funding the managed
detection service and the annual penetration test. Any spend above GBP 25,000
requires a second signature from the CFO.
