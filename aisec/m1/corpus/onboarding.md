# New Starter Onboarding — ACME Internal

Welcome to ACME Logistics. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Day one
Collect your laptop from IT on the 3rd floor. Your account is created automatically
from the HR record, so sign in with the temporary password emailed to your personal
address and change it immediately.

## Equipment
Standard issue is a 14" laptop, a dock and two monitors. Requests for additional
hardware go through your line manager, not the IT helpdesk directly.

## Holiday
25 days plus bank holidays, pro-rata in your first year. Book through the HR portal
with at least two weeks' notice.

## Who to ask
People questions go to Priya Raman (HR Lead). System access questions go to the
IT service desk. Anything security related goes to Tom Whitfield (Head of Security).
